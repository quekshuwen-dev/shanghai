Strict vegetarian requirement: Eggs and dairy are okay. No meat, fish, seafood, meat/fish broth, oyster sauce or lard. Ask about soup bases, sauces and cooking oil. Do not assume a vegetable or tofu dish is vegetarian.

Transport: For six passengers and luggage, arrange a 7–9-seat MPV for airport transfers. Use Didi MPV for shorter city journeys. For Suzhou and Wuxi, take high-speed trains and arrange a local MPV to meet you at the station.

Flight details: Latest outbound timing provided: arrival in Shanghai at 2:25pm on 8 December. Return flight: 8:20am on 17 December. Check your airline confirmation for flight numbers, terminal and Singapore arrival time.

Ticket and booking guide

1. Shantang Street canal boat · 13 December

Where: Official Chuxin Dock ticket counter (初心码头), No. 173 Shantang Street. Buy on arrival rather than prepaying an unfamiliar seller.

  • Published option: ¥50 sightseeing boat towards Tiger Hill, around 30 minutes.
  • Published option: ¥80 Shangtang River return ride, around 1 hour.
  • Ask which route is operating, whether the ticket is one-way or return, and the last departure.

Driver address: 苏州市姑苏区山塘街173号 初心码头

2. Bund Huangpu River night cruise · 14 December

  • Preferred channel: official Huangpu River Cruise (浦江游览) website or official WeChat/Alipay mini-program.
  • English booking alternatives: Trip.com, Klook or Traveloka.
  • Confirm exact pier, sailing time, indoor seating, child height rules, senior concessions and passport conditions before paying.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before sailing.
  • A published off-peak fare has been ¥120 per person, but treat this as indicative only and verify the actual fare.

3. High-speed trains · Suzhou and Wuxi

  • Official booking: China Railway 12306 English website.
  • Tickets are generally released 15 days ahead; check the actual release calendar.
  • Suzhou: choose Suzhou Railway Station (苏州站), not Suzhou North.
  • Wuxi: choose Wuxi Railway Station (无锡站) for the planned driver pickup.
  • Choose services with a generous buffer. Do not book a train that leaves immediately after the planned sightseeing finish.

4. Disneyland and Lingshan

  • Check official Disneyland opening hours, ride height restrictions, showtimes and hotel guest entry arrangements closer to the date.
  • For Lingshan, confirm admission, internal shuttle, show schedule and foreign-passport child/senior concession rules before purchasing.
